Refund Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

We want you to feel safe trying a paid plan. This policy explains when refunds apply.

1. 7-day money-back on your first purchase

If you are not satisfied with your first paid purchase, email us within 7 days of that purchase and we will refund it in full. This applies once, to your first purchase of a paid plan.

2. After that — no pro-rata refunds

For renewals and any later purchases, and after the 7-day window on your first purchase, plans are non-refundable. If you cancel or stop using the Service, you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for, but we do not refund unused days on a pro-rata basis.

3. How to claim a refund

  1. Email dipsgos7@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Refund request”.
  2. Include your order/payment ID (shown in your account under payment history) and the reason.
  3. We will confirm and process eligible refunds to your original payment method.

4. Processing time

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days and issued to your original payment method. Your bank or card/UPI provider may take a little longer to show the amount.

5. Exceptions

We may decline refunds in cases of abuse (for example, repeated buy-and-refund cycles) or where access controls or these terms have been violated.

6. Contact

Questions about refunds: dipsgos7@gmail.com.
